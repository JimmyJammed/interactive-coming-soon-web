## The part people get wrong

**Changing things does not make it yours to sell.**

Swapping the colours, the fonts, the copy, the icons, the 3D avatar, the
animation timing, or renaming the files does **not** turn this into a new
template that you can put on a marketplace. Make all of those changes for
*your site* — that is encouraged. Do not make them in order to sell another
template.

The test isn't how much you changed. It's whether the result is a **finished
site someone operates**, or a **development resource other people can
download**. The first is fine. The second is not.
